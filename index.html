Operational Flow

How your kiosk currently works end-to-end.

STEP 1
Provision device as Device Owner and launch iPlay Kiosk portal.
STEP 2
Open admin panel, scan BLE, and bind matching iPlay ESP32 firmware.
STEP 3
Activate paid key permanently or claim trial key for 30-minute session.
STEP 4
Run portal with whitelist + lock policies while ESP32 reports coin pulses.

Important

Your setup is intentionally offline. This is practical for kiosk operation, but advanced users with root-level access can still tamper at OS level. For production, keep Device Owner policy enforced and physical access controlled.
